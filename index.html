About

Coach Downey Huynh

I started volleyball late and had no roadmap — clubs, training, or how to play in college. I went from not playing freshman year to competing at Orange Coast College and the University of Jamestown, where I later coached as assistant and head coach.

Now I help athletes and parents skip the confusion: strong fundamentals, real game skills, and the guidance I wish I had starting out.
